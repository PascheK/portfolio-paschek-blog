> Devlog #0 is the one before the game exists. No gameplay yet: just where the idea comes from, what it is, and why I'm writing about it before it's finished. A video version is on its way. I'm still writing the script.

## 1. It started with Blender

At work I use 3D all the time, mostly through Unreal Engine. I was surrounded by 3D files and I had never learned the tool that makes the assets. So I decided to finally learn **Blender**, just for fun.

I followed several tutorials. That worked well, up to a point: after a while I had a folder of exercises, and what I needed was a **real project** to put my Blender assets into. Something concrete, with a goal, instead of one more donut.

## 2. So I'll make a game

The obvious project for a pile of 3D assets is a **3D game**. The honest part comes next: I'm **not** going to make my dream game.

I've been here before. My [2D survival RPG](/en/blog/unity-survival-rpg) was ambitious, and it is now on hold. This time I want something small enough to finish, and silly enough that I won't take myself too seriously.

## 3. The pitch

The game is a bit goofy, in the spirit of **Goat Simulator**, mixed with the adventure side of **Spyro**.

And it stars my hamster, **Bouboulle**.

- You play a hamster who has to **explore a world**.
- A storm blew away all the **cherries**, and you have to find every one of them.
- It is meant to be an adventure you wander through, with a stupid sense of humour.

That's the whole pitch for now. If it makes me smile when I describe it, it's probably the right size.

## 4. How I plan to not give up

The real risk with a side project is not the code, it's motivation. So I gave myself goals that don't depend on the game being done:

- **Document the work**, as I go.
- **Write devlogs**, like this one, so each step has a deadline of its own.
- And if I manage to build a small **community** around it, maybe that's the motivation I need to finish what I started.

Writing in public is the commitment device. If you're reading this, you're already part of it.

## 5. What's next

- Finish the **script** of the video version of this devlog.
- Keep learning Blender by building the assets of the game.
- Share the first things I make, in **Devlog #1**.
