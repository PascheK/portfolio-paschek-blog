## Context

I use language models every day, for code, for writing and for thinking through problems. The existing chat interfaces were either too generic or gave me little control over the context. I wanted my own: a personal AI workspace that can read my notes, my code and my documents.

## Why a desktop app

I wanted an app that could manage files on the operating system directly, which a web app cannot do. That is why I moved from a web interface to Electron.

## What I built

- **Desktop app** built with Electron and Next.js, with a macOS build through Electron Builder.
- **Chat with several conversation contexts**, using the AI SDK and OpenAI models.
- **Automatic analysis of files and projects**, with document reading and embeddings.
- **Local RAG**: retrieval over my own documents, with Drizzle ORM on Neon or SQLite.
- **Interface**: responsive, with light animations.

## Stack

Electron, Next.js, TypeScript, AI SDK / OpenAI, Drizzle ORM, Neon / SQLite, Tailwind CSS.

## Status

It is a personal project and I do not use it day to day anymore. It remains a good record of what I wanted to explore: AI integration, document retrieval and desktop packaging.
