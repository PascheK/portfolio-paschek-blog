## Context

At UNOPS, my goal is to build tools that let teams present the work they have done. Beryl is one of them: a web experience that works like an interactive PowerPoint, where you scroll instead of clicking through slides. It tells the story of Hurricane Beryl in three phases: before, during and after.

## My role

I designed the narrative flow and built the whole front end.

## What I built

- **Three-phase story**: overlays are triggered from a central configuration file, so the narrative can be edited without touching the components.
- **Video synced to scroll**: a `useScrollVideo` hook ties playback to the scroll position, and a `FullScrollSection` component orchestrates each phase. Videos are preloaded to keep the animation stable.
- **Smooth motion**: Lenis for smooth scrolling, GSAP ScrollTrigger and Framer Motion for transitions and overlays.
- **Live data**: ArcGIS StoryMaps and dashboards embedded directly in the story.
- **Report**: the journey ends with a downloadable PDF report for stakeholders.

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion, Lenis, ArcGIS StoryMaps and Dashboards.

## Result

The teams loved these tools and presented them at a conference in Geneva.
