## Context

For a course, I had to present a history lesson on the 1929 crisis. Instead of slides, I built a website that carries the whole presentation: it is the support I used for the lesson.

## What I built

- **Structured pages**: objectives, content and methodology for the lesson.
- **Chapters**: a navigation organised by chapter, designed for a long 50-minute session.
- **Teaching material**: presentations, summaries and sources.
- **Writing in MDX**: the content is plain text files, so editing the lesson does not require touching the code.
- **Responsive design** for any screen.

## Stack

Next.js, React, Markdown / MDX, Tailwind CSS.

## What is next

I would like to turn it into a reusable course-site template, so that preparing the next lesson means writing content rather than building a site.
