## Context

The UNOPS teams carried out a geospatial analysis of how armed conflict has affected buildings in Khartoum, Sudan, between 2023 and 2025. They needed a way to present that work clearly to partners and decision-makers. My part was to build the interactive report that tells the story, using the starter I made for UNOPS.

## My role

I designed and built the report: the structure of the story, the map and the before/after comparisons. The analysis and the data come from the UNOPS teams.

## What I built

- **A two-phase story**: pre-conflict (establishing the baseline) and post-conflict (assessing the damage, then planning recovery). A menu switches the phase and loads the matching story in the map.
- **Key figures up front**: 256,123 buildings analysed and 4,687 critical infrastructures identified, of which 389 were identified as damaged through remote analysis. These figures come from the UNOPS analysis.
- **Before/after satellite comparison**: a slider to compare the same hospital or school in 2023 and in 2025.
- **An interactive map** built with Mapbox GL, where buildings are coloured by category (education, health, power, waste, water) and by status (damaged, undamaged, unknown), with a legend, a guided story mode and a fullscreen view.
- **A partners section** crediting the organisations that collaborated on the analysis.

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Mapbox GL, GSAP, Recharts, Radix UI, Tailwind CSS, Vercel. It starts from [create-unops-starter](/en/projects/unops-registry).

## Result

Like the other interactive reports I built for UNOPS, it works as an interactive PowerPoint. The teams loved these tools and presented them at a conference in Geneva.
